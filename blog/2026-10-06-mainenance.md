## MATLAB on Hyak Research Clusters

We have recently made improvements to the user experience for using MATLAB on our clusters.

MATLAB is available on both UW on-premises research computing clusters: **Hyak Klone** and **Tillicum**. These systems, operated by UWIT Research Computing, provide scalable high-performance computing resources for a wide range of research workloads.

After purchasing a MATLAB license, you can activate MATLAB on Hyak Klone or Tillicum for large-scale computing workflows.

[Learn more about using MATLAB on Hyak](https://hyak.uw.edu/docs/guides/applications/matlab/).

**UW students:** Students who pay the Student Technology Fee can access Hyak Klone through the [Research Computing Club](https://depts.washington.edu/uwrcc/) for independent research and use MATLAB on Hyak at no additional cost.
